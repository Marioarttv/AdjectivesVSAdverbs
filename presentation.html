Phase 3: Reproduction

How to make an ADVERB

ADJECTIVE + -ly = ADVERB

careful + -ly = carefully

quick + -ly = quickly

slow + -ly = slowly

happy + -ly = happily

Phase 4: Awareness

Our Grammar Rules 📋

ADJECTIVES

Describe: NOUNS

Answer: WHAT KIND?

Position: BEFORE the noun

Examples:

  • The slow turtle
  • The happy girl
  • A quick breakfast

ADVERBS

Describe: VERBS

Answer: HOW?

Position: AFTER the verb

Examples:

  • moves slowly
  • sings happily
  • eats quickly
Adjective + -ly = Adverb
Phase 4: Awareness

⚠️ Watch Out!

Irregular Forms

good → well

❌ NOT "goodly"!

fast → fast

✓ stays the same!

✓ He is a good player. He plays well.

✓ The fast car drives fast.

Quick Reference Sheet 📄

ADJECTIVES

→ Describe NOUNS

→ BEFORE the noun

→ Answer: WHAT KIND?

Examples:

  • slow, fast
  • happy, sad
  • beautiful, ugly
  • big, small
  • good, bad

ADVERBS

→ Describe VERBS

→ AFTER the verb

→ Answer: HOW?

Examples:

  • slowly, quickly
  • happily, sadly
  • beautifully
  • carefully
  • well (not goodly!)
Adjective + -ly = Adverb

⚠️ Irregular: good → well | fast → fast
