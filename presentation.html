What are ADJECTIVES?

ADJECTIVES

✓ Describe NOUNS

✓ Describe things, people, or animals

✓ Tell us: WHAT KIND?

• The slow turtle

• The green turtle

• The big cheetah

• The happy girl

What are ADVERBS?

ADVERBS

✓ Describe VERBS

✓ Describe actions

✓ Tell us: HOW?

• The turtle moves slowly

• The cheetah runs quickly

• The girl sings beautifully

• The student walks carefully

How to make an ADVERB

ADJECTIVE + -ly = ADVERB

careful + -ly = carefully

quick + -ly = quickly

slow + -ly = slowly

happy + -ly = happily

Our Grammar Rules 📋

ADJECTIVES

Describe: NOUNS

(people, things, animals)

Position:
BEFORE the noun

Examples:

  • The slow turtle
  • The happy girl
  • A quick breakfast

ADVERBS

Describe: VERBS

(actions)

Position:
AFTER the verb

Examples:

  • moves slowly
  • sings happily
  • eats quickly

The Rule

Adjective + -ly = Adverb

careful → carefully

quick → quickly

slow → slowly

beautiful → beautifully

⚠️ Watch Out!

Irregular Forms

good → well

❌ NOT "goodly"!

fast → fast

✓ stays the same!

✓ He is a good player.

✓ He plays well.

✓ The fast car drives fast.

Where do they go in a sentence?

ADJECTIVE → BEFORE the noun

The slow turtle

ADVERB → AFTER the verb

The turtle moves slowly

Memory Help! 🧠

Adjectives describe nouns
→ They tell us WHAT KIND

Adverbs describe verbs
→ They tell us HOW

What did we learn today?

Summary:

  • Adjectives describe nouns (WHAT KIND?)
  • Adverbs describe verbs (HOW?)
  • Most adverbs: adjective + -ly
  • Position: adjective before noun, adverb after verb
  • Irregular: good → well, fast → fast

Quick Reference Sheet

ADJECTIVES

→ BEFORE noun

→ WHAT KIND?

Examples:

  • slow, fast
  • happy, sad
  • beautiful, ugly
  • big, small
  • good, bad

ADVERBS

→ AFTER verb

→ HOW?

Examples:

  • slowly, fast
  • happily, sadly
  • beautifully
  • carefully, quickly
  • well (not goodly!)
Adjective + -ly = Adverb
